Beef Lasagna

Description

Standard beef lasagna with ricotta, mozzarella, and tomato-based meat sauce. Structured for layered assembly and oven baking.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 3/4 lb ground beef
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 lb mozzarella cheese
  • 28 oz crushed tomatoes

Steps

  1. Brown sausage and beef with onions and garlic in a pot.
  2. Add tomatoes and simmer for 60 minutes.
  3. Boil noodles until al dente, then drain.
  4. Layer meat sauce, noodles, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  5. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 50 minutes (25 covered, 25 uncovered).
